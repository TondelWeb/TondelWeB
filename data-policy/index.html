Data Handling Policy

Draft, pending professional review

This policy applies to every Tondelweb service and is part of the Master Service Agreement.

1. What we collect, by service

ServiceWhat we receiveWhat we don't want
CatalogYour Shopify product export, your supplier spreadsheet(s), and your approvalsCustomer, order or payment data
FormsForm page URLs, alert contacts, our own fictional test submissions, the test notification emails in our test inbox, and screenshots of checksWebsite logins, real lead data
BidsYour capability profile (service area, crew size, insurance, bonding, set-aside status) and your feedback on digestsBid pricing, proposals, tax IDs
WebsitesContent and assets you provide for your site projectPasswords sent by email (we use a shared password-manager item instead)
EveryoneNames, business emails, invoices and payment status (card details stay with Stripe; we never see full card numbers)

If data we don't want arrives by mistake, we delete it and tell you:

  • within 24 hours of noticing for Forms (for example a real lead that was misrouted);
  • within 5 business days for other services.

2. How we use it

Only to provide the service you ordered, to bill you and to support you. We don't:

  • sell or rent your data;
  • share it with other clients;
  • reuse it for anyone else.

Supplier pricing and capability profiles are treated as confidential.

3. Where it's stored

  • Client files: in a separate folder per client, on a Mac with full-disk encryption (FileVault) turned on. Not in personal cloud drives.
  • Forms check results and screenshots: on a Tondelweb server (DigitalOcean, US region), with each agency's data in its own folder. The server allows SSH-key login only and has automatic security updates.
  • Bids: profiles and digests on the same encrypted Mac or server, in a separate per-client folder.
  • Email and calendar: in Google (Gmail, Google Calendar).
  • Payments: in Stripe.

4. Who can see it

  • Lucas L, the owner.
  • Contractors, if we use them, under a written confidentiality agreement and only for the clients they work on.
  • The service providers in section 6, only to run their part.

5. AI-assisted processing

  • We use software, including AI tools, to compare files, read public documents and draft reports.
  • A person reviews results before anything is delivered.
  • Catalog's proposed fixes come from rule-based checks that cite your supplier file. Bids quotes are checked word for word against the source notice.
  • AI service used: Anthropic Claude.

6. Service providers (subprocessors)

  • Google (Gmail, Calendar)
  • Stripe (payments)
  • Formspree (website contact form)
  • Cal.com (booking, if used)
  • GitHub (website hosting)
  • DigitalOcean (server)
  • testmail.app (Forms test inbox)
  • Resend (automated alert email, once set up)
  • Anthropic (AI processing)

We'll update this list when it changes.

7. How long we keep it

DataKept
Catalog filesDuring the service; deleted within 30 days after it ends, with an email confirming deletion. You can ask us to keep only the history of accepted and rejected changes.
Forms results and screenshotsUp to 90 days. Monthly reports: for the term of the service.
Bids profiles and digestsDuring the service; deleted within 30 days after it ends.
Invoices and payment recordsAs long as tax law requires (ask your tax professional; often several years).

8. Your choices

You can ask what we hold, or ask us to delete it sooner, by emailing Lucas.Lagido@gmail.com. We'll answer within 10 business days. Deleting data may mean we can't continue the service.

9. If something goes wrong

If we learn that your data was accessed by someone who shouldn't have it, we'll:

  • tell you promptly, normally within 72 hours of confirming it;
  • share what we know and what we're doing about it;
  • meet any legal notification duties that apply.
