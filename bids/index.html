FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is this different from free SAM.gov alerts?

Free alerts match codes, and you still read everything. We read the notice and its documents, check each one against your profile, quote the evidence, and list exclusions. We also cover more than federal sources, as listed above.

How fresh is the data?

We check our sources every morning. Each digest shows when each source was last checked. Urgent items (deadline under 10 days) and amendments come as separate alerts.

What do you need from me?

A 15-minute profile: base city, travel radius, largest job you can staff, insurance limits, bonding, experience, and any set-aside status (you tell us; we don't certify it).

Can I share the digest?

It's for your company's own use. Please don't resell or redistribute it.
