How it works

Four steps. Nothing runs until you sign off.

Step 1

Pick 5 sites

One lead form each. We write the tests from the public pages. No logins, no plugins to buy.

Step 2

Route test entries

You add a 5-minute routing rule so our clearly marked test entries go only to our inbox, never to the firm's staff or CRM. We check it together first.

Step 3

We test every 4 hours

Submit, confirm the success message, confirm the email arrived. Problems reach you with a screenshot, the time and the failing step.

Step 4

A report per site you can forward to the firm: checks run, incidents, time to fix, coverage.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will test entries reach the firm's staff or CRM?

No. Every test carries a secret marker, and your routing rule sends marked emails only to our test inbox. Nothing runs on a schedule until we've done one supervised test together and you've confirmed staff and the CRM didn't see it.

Do you need access to the sites?

No. We use the public form like a visitor. You install the routing rule; we never log in.

What does "arrived" prove?

That the page, form, server, WordPress, the notification and the site's own email sending all worked, end to end. It doesn't prove the firm's mailbox address is correct; an optional mailbox-copy setup covers that.

What happens during a redesign?

Tell us 2 business days ahead. We pause those sites and re-check after launch. One re-baseline per site per quarter is included.

Which form plugins work?

Gravity Forms, WPForms, Contact Form 7, Elementor Forms and Fluent Forms in the pilot; others case by case. Embedded third-party forms are checked front-end only.
