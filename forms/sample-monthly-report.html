How to read this report

  • Full arrival: we submitted the form with a clearly marked test identity and confirmed the notification email arrived in our test inbox through the site's own email setup. This covers the page, the form, the server, WordPress, the notification and the site's email sending. It doesn't cover the firm's own mailbox (a separate copy goes there).
  • Front-end only: we load the page and confirm the form and its fields are present, but don't submit it (for example because it has a CAPTCHA, which we never try to get past, or because test entries can't be kept out of a connected system).
  • Working is the share of completed checks that passed. Short gaps between checks (about every 4 hours) aren't covered.
  • Test entries go only to our test inbox. Our checks never log in to or change the site; fixes are made by Demo Legal Marketing.
