Schedule A: Tondelweb Catalog (supplier-file checks for Shopify catalogs)

A1. Service. We compare the supplier file(s) you provide with the Shopify product export you provide, and deliver:

  • (a) a report of issues found;
  • (b) proposed changes, each citing the supplier file, row and cell that supports it;
  • (c) after you approve changes, a CSV import file containing only the approved changes.

A2. Plans.

  • Audit: up to 100 SKUs against one supplier file, delivered within 5 business days. Price: $250, one-time.
  • Weekly check: 1 supplier, 1 store, up to 1,000 monitored SKUs and up to 50 changed rows a week. Reports within 2 business days of each file. Price: $399/month.
  • Turnaround counts from when we have all the required files.

A3. No store access.

  • We don't log into, access or write to your store, and we never ask for store credentials.
  • You approve each change and import the file yourself. You're responsible for what you publish.
  • We recommend exporting a backup before every import.

A4. What we never do. We never invent or supply, on our own, product specifications, certifications or safety listings, voltage, compatibility information, prices or inventory. Where the supplier file doesn't state a value, we flag it for your decision.

A5. Accuracy. We don't warrant that your supplier's data is accurate.

  • Correction: if a change we propose isn't supported by the cited supplier cell, we correct it within 1 business day of you telling us, at no charge.

A6. Guarantees and credits.

  • Audit refund: if an audit finds fewer than 5 actionable issues in the SKUs checked, we refund the audit fee in full.
  • Missed weekly turnaround: that week's share of the fee is credited against your next invoice.
  • Weekly overages: we tell you first. You choose to carry the extra rows to the next week, or pay $1 per extra row.

A7. What you send. Only product data: supplier files and Shopify product exports. Don't send customer, order or payment data. If any arrives by mistake, we delete it and tell you.

Schedule B: Tondelweb Forms (lead-form monitoring for agencies)

B1. Service.

  • Checks: we run automated checks on the website forms listed in the Order Form, about every 4 hours. A check:
    • loads the page;
    • fills in the agreed form with a clearly marked test identity;
    • submits it;
    • confirms the on-page confirmation; and
    • for "Full arrival" sites, confirms the notification email reached our test inbox.
  • Alerts go to the contacts in the Order Form.
  • Reports: we send a monthly report per site, which you may forward to your clients.

B2. Coverage labels. Each site is labelled:

  • "Full arrival": submitted, and arrival confirmed; or
  • "Front-end only": page and form checked, not submitted. For example, the site has a CAPTCHA or an embedded third-party form, or test entries can't be kept out of a connected system.

We tell you when a site's label changes.

B3. Not included. We don't:

  • repair, edit or log in to any website;
  • bypass or solve CAPTCHAs or other bot protection;
  • make test purchases or checkouts;
  • provide visual or design QA;
  • provide emergency 24/7 human response.

Automated alerts are sent 24/7. We review alerts and answer messages within 1 business day (Monday–Friday, Eastern Time).

B4. Your authorization. You confirm that:

  • (a) you have authority from each website owner listed to authorize automated test submissions on the listed forms, at the agreed frequency, using the test identity in the Order Form;
  • (b) you'll tell us promptly if that authority ends; and
  • (c) we may rely on your authorization.

You can remove a site anytime by email. We stop its checks within 1 business day.

B5. Test entries and routing.

  • Before scheduled checks start on a site, you set up the agreed routing (a form-plugin rule or our routing file), so test entries go only to our test inbox and never to site-owner staff, CRMs or other systems.
  • We run one supervised test with you. Scheduled checks start only after you confirm it worked ("sign-off").
  • If a test entry reaches anyone else, tell us. We'll pause that site until it's fixed.

B6. Redesigns.

  • Give us 2 business days' notice of redesigns, theme changes or form rebuilds. We pause those sites and re-baseline after launch.
  • One re-baseline per site per quarter is included; extra ones are $30 per site.
  • Without notice, failures caused by changes may be reported as "test needs update".

B7. Fees.

  • Pilot: $150 for up to 5 sites for 30 days, paid in advance. If we can't get at least one site to "Full arrival" within 10 business days of receiving your completed onboarding information, we refund the pilot fee in full.
  • Monitoring pack: $199/month per 5 sites, plus $40/month per extra site.

B8. Limits and credits.

  • What a check can't see: checks run at intervals and test only the listed forms. Failures between checks, on other forms or in recipients' own mailboxes may not be detected. "Full arrival" confirms delivery to our test inbox, not to the site owner's own mailbox.
  • Credits: if, because of a problem on our side, checks don't run on a site for more than 24 hours in a row, or a site gets more than one false alert in a calendar month, you get a credit of 10% of that month's fee per event, up to 50% of that month's fee.

B9. Data specific to Forms.

  • Our checks use only fictional test data.
  • If real personal data reaches us by mistake (for example through misrouting), we delete it within 24 hours of noticing and tell you.
  • We keep check results and screenshots for up to 90 days, and monthly reports for the term of the service.

Schedule C: Tondelweb Bids (filtered government cleaning-bid digest)

C1. Service.

  • Digest: a weekly digest, plus occasional alerts, of government commercial-cleaning opportunities in Texas, from the sources listed in C2.
  • Screening: each opportunity is screened against the capability profile you give us. You're responsible for the profile's accuracy.

C2. Sources.

  • Monitored now: (1) SAM.gov Contract Opportunities, federal notices with a Texas place of performance.
  • Added later: Texas ESBD and named local portals, only after permission is confirmed and with notice to you.
  • Not all contracts: we do not monitor all government contracts. Agencies also publish elsewhere, by invitation, or under other classifications, and some work is reserved by law for particular suppliers.

C3. What we don't do.

  • We don't write or review proposals, prepare pricing or submit bids.
  • We don't certify your eligibility for any set-aside, size standard, license or registration. We show what each notice says; you decide whether you qualify.

C4. Confirm on the official notice. You must confirm every detail, including deadlines, site-visit requirements and eligibility, on the official notice before deciding to bid. Sources update on their own schedules. Each digest shows when each source was last checked. This isn't a real-time service.

C5. Corrections and credits (sole remedy). This section applies if we:

  • leave out an opportunity that was published on a listed source, matched your profile, and was still open; or
  • state a deadline, site-visit requirement or dealbreaker incorrectly.

If that happens:

  • we tell you within one business day of learning of it;
  • we correct it; and
  • we credit you one week of fees per verified error, up to one month of fees in any month.

C6. Fees. $49 for a two-week trial, then $149/month if you continue.

C7. Use. The digest is for your company's internal use. Don't resell, publish or redistribute it.

Schedule D: Tondelweb Websites (custom websites)

D1. Service. We design and build a website for your business as described in the Order Form, then host and look after it on the monthly plan.

  • Timeline: your website is up and running within 3 business days, with a first draft to review within 1 business day. Both are counted from when your first month is paid and you've sent your logo, photos and service details.
  • Before you pay anything, we may show you a free demo of a possible design. A demo is only a sample. It isn't a delivered website, and it has no live data.

D2. No build fee. There's no separate charge to build the site. Your first month ($50) is paid by Stripe invoice before work starts.

D3. Monthly plan.

  • $50/month. It covers the website, hosting, the security certificate (SSL), help managing your domain, and reasonable small edits on request (text, photos, hours, prices, contact details), with no set hour limit. New pages or redesigns are quoted separately.
  • The first month is paid before work starts (D2). After that it's billed monthly, runs month to month and can be cancelled any month (section 5.2). No long-term contract.

D4. Google Ads (optional).

  • Setup $300–500 one-time, plus management $300–500/month, the exact amounts in the Order Form.
  • Ad spend is paid by you directly to Google and isn't part of our fees.
  • We don't guarantee clicks, calls, leads or sales from ads (section 9).

D5. What you provide.

  • You provide the text, photos, logos and business details for the site, and you confirm you have the right to use them.
  • You review the site before launch.
  • We're not responsible for delays caused by missing content.

D6. Changes before launch. The plan includes one round of changes after you see the first draft. More rounds before launch are quoted separately.

D7. Domain.

  • Your domain name is registered in your own account and belongs to you.
  • If we help set it up, you give us only the access needed, and you can remove it anytime.

D8. Ownership and handover. For the site's design and code, this section replaces section 8.1.

  • Always yours: your own content (the text, photos, logos and business details you give us) and your domain (D7).
  • The site we build (its design and code) stays ours until you've paid 6 monthly fees. While your plan is active, you may use it for your business.
  • After 6 paid monthly fees, the site's design and code made for you become yours. Any of our reusable code or templates inside it are licensed to you to use for that site, without end. If you cancel after that, we send you a copy of the site files within 5 business days of the end of your last paid month.
  • If you cancel before 6 paid monthly fees, you can keep the site by paying a one-time buyout of $300. We then send the site files within 5 business days.
    • Without the buyout, we take the site offline at the end of your last paid month.
    • You keep your own content and your domain.
  • Either way, once we stop hosting it, moving it to another host is up to you. Monthly fees already paid aren't refunded (section 5.2).

D9. No ranking or traffic promises. We build to good practice, including fast pages, phone-friendly layouts and basic search setup. We don't guarantee search rankings, traffic, leads or sales (section 9).

D10. Late payment. If a monthly fee is unpaid 14 days after it's due, we may take the site offline after emailing you (section 4.3). We put it back once you've paid.
