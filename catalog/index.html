The problem

Every new spec sheet is a new chance to publish a mistake.

01

Supplier files change constantly

New wattages, new barcodes, new SKUs. Someone has to compare them line by line against what's live.

02

Sync apps move data. They don't check it.

Copying a supplier's numbers into Shopify is easy. Spotting when they contradict your titles, options and other listings isn't.

03

Wrong specs get noticed, by the wrong people

A bulb listed as 3000K that arrives at 4000K means a return, a bad review, or a feed disapproval.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you need access to my Shopify store?

No. You export your products (Products → Export) and import our file (Products → Import). You stay in full control.

What happens to my files?

They're kept in one encrypted folder, processed with AI-assisted tools under our written data terms, and deleted within 30 days after we stop working together. We only need product data, never customer or order data.

What if a proposed fix is wrong?

Every fix shows its source, so you can check it in seconds. If a fix we deliver isn't supported by the supplier file, we correct it within one business day at no charge.

We already use an app to sync supplier feeds.

Keep it. Sync apps move data into your store. We check that data, with the source for every fix, so mistakes don't go live.

Is this only for lighting?

We started with lighting because its specs (watts, lumens, color temperature, base type, dimensions) can be checked precisely. If you sell another spec-heavy product line, get in touch.
